Quick start

Your first 5 minutes

Brand new? Do these five things in order. You can't break anything — nothing changes until you press a Save button.

  1. Open the dashboard — type emorywoods.vercel.app/jr-dashboard into the bar at the very top of your internet browser, type the access code, press Enter.
  2. Click a building on the map. The list on the right now shows only that building.
  3. Click any apartment in the list. A box opens with its details. Look around, then press Cancel.
  4. Type a tenant's last name in the search box at the top. Click their name in the results.
  5. Click Create Document, choose Brochure, then click Generate Brochure. The brochure opens, ready to print.

Renewal alerts — how they work

For Occupied units on a 12-Month Lease, the dashboard watches the Lease To date (or works it out from Lease From).

WhenYou'll see
More than 30 days leftNothing — all good.
30 days or lessLease ends in 12d + listed in Renewals
Date has passedLease expired

Month-to-month and Notice units never show renewal alerts.

Renew a lease
  1. Open Renewals and click the unit. A banner shows the end date.
  2. Change Last Lease Renewal / Lease From to the new lease start.
  3. Check Lease To moved one year on. If not, type it in.
  4. Update Rent if it changed → Save Unit.
the unit leaves the Renewals tab.

How the money is worked out — you don't have to do it

Move-in Oct 20 → tenant pays for the 12 gold days out of 31
  1. Monthly bill = rent + water/sewer (plus $25 for a pet over 25 lbs).
  2. First month — the tenant only pays for the days they live there, counting move-in day.
  3. Pet? A one-time $300 pet fee is added.
  4. Moving in on one of the last 3 days of a month? Next month's full rent is added too, so they don't pay twice in a few days.

Every document opens in a new tab at the top of your browser. Here's what to do next.

Nothing opened? Your browser blocked it
One-time fix

Browsers sometimes stop new tabs from opening, to protect you from ads. You only need to allow it once.

  1. Look at the right end of the address bar (the long bar at the top). A small icon with a red ✕ appears.
  2. Click that icon.
  3. Click "Always allow pop-ups and redirects from emorywoods.vercel.app", then Done.
  4. Go back to the dashboard and click the document button again.

Make your own list or checklist

Need a sign-up sheet, an inspection checklist, or a page for meter readings? The ▦ Table button makes a printable grid: the apartments you pick go down the left side, and the headings you type go across the top.

1 · Select Units
  1. Click ▦ Table.
  2. Tick units, or search then Select All.
  3. Clear unticks all.
  4. Click Next (n selected).
☑ 2194ND01☑ 2194ND02☐ 2194ND03
2 · Build Columns
  1. Rename Column 1 by typing over it.
  2. + Add Column for more.
  3. + Sub-column splits a column (e.g. Smoke detector → Bedroom / Hall).
  4. Optional: type into the cells below.
  5. Remove / ✕ deletes. Then Preview.
3 · Preview & Print
  1. Pick Portrait or Landscape.
  2. Click Print / Export.
  3. Choose a printer or Save as PDF.
  4. Back to change anything.

The big list view

Prefer a list like Excel instead of the map? Every apartment, one per line, with every detail side by side.

  1. At the top, click Table in the Map | Table switch. (Not the ▦ Table button — that one makes your own list.)
  2. Choose Units (apartments) or Carports (parking) just above the list.
  3. Click any line to open that apartment's details.
  4. Click Map to go back to the map.

Putting the list in order (sorting)

Click a heading — e.g. Rent — and the list is put in order by rent, lowest first. Click the same heading again for highest first. A small ▲ or ▼ shows which way.

Showing only some lines (filtering)

Under each heading is a small box:

  • Boxes with ▾ are drop-down lists — click and pick, e.g. 3BR. Columns like Paint or Tour offer Yes or No.
  • Boxes that say Filter… are for typing — e.g. part of a tenant's name.
  • Clear all turns every filter off again.

FAQ & troubleshooting

I changed something but it didn't stick.
You closed the window without Save Unit. Reopen, redo, save.
A document didn't open.
Your browser blocked it. Follow the pictures on page 16 — it's a one-time fix.
The document shows the wrong name or date.
Fix the boxes in the document form before generating — or fix the unit's Future Tenant, then start again.
"Moved In?" or "Move Out" is greyed out.
Moved In? needs a Future Tenant name. Move Out needs status Notice and a Tenant Name.
A unit is missing from my list.
Click ✕ Clear on the gold strip and on the filter buttons, and set All Types back. Or just search for it.
Can I add or delete an apartment?
No — the unit list is fixed. Ask the site administrator.
Can I undo?
There's no undo button. Open the unit and set the values back, then save.
Asked for the access code again.
New browser, cleared data, or the code was changed. Ask your manager.
